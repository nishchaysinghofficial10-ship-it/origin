# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is about honest, verifiable work. The same standard applies to how
we treat each other: be direct, be accurate, and assume the other person is
acting in good faith.

**Expected:** clear technical disagreement backed by evidence; accepting a
correction gracefully; crediting others' work; patience with newcomers who are
learning the codebase.

**Not accepted:** harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, sustained
disruption, publishing others' private information, or sexualised language or
imagery in any project space.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour through the repository's private security or
maintainer contact. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not
follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences decided by other
maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or reject contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions are explained to
the person affected.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
