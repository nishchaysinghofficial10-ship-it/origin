06 / HONEST BOUNDARIES

What ORIGIN
does not claim.

Trust starts by naming the edge of the evidence.

  • 01
    Not an all-powerful autonomous scientist

    General mode performs cited public-web synthesis; only registered computational domains run locally verified experiments.

  • 02
    Not kernel-grade isolation

    Experiment confinement is user-space. A public worker requires an additional container boundary.

  • 03
    Not guaranteed truth

    Search results and model output remain untrusted. Citations make claims inspectable, but synthesis is not experimental proof.

  • 04
    Not unlimited

    Every tester, paid provider request, search, output, and wall-clock duration has a hard operator-controlled limit.

  • 05
    Not personalized professional advice

    Medical, legal, and financial topics receive general research context with explicit uncertainty—not individual instructions.
